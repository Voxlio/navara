About Navara

Built Around Marine Operations. Driven by What Comes Next.

Navara Offshore is built on a practical understanding of marine operations and a forward-looking view of where the industry is going.

Perspective

Marine Expertise With a Wider Perspective

Navara Offshore is a marine and offshore company providing services across vessel operations, fleet management, chartering, offshore logistics, procurement, technical support and maritime technology.

We understand that effective marine operations depend on people, planning, maintenance, logistics, suppliers, ports, regulations, technology and commercial decisions working together. That wider perspective informs how we support our clients.

Our Approach

We Look Beyond the Vessel

Before supporting an assignment, we consider the operating environment, the client's requirements and the resources needed to deliver effectively.

Our approach connects operational, commercial and technical considerations to support better outcomes.
