Our capabilities

Marine capability, connected for the way you operate

Integrated marine and offshore support across vessel operations, chartering, logistics, procurement, technical coordination and maritime technology.

Marine operations

Keeping vessels working

Fleet management

Support to help clients coordinate vessel-related activities, resources and service requirements across their operating environment.

  • Vessel scheduling and coordination
  • Performance and utilisation oversight
  • Operational planning
  • Compliance and documentation

Vessel management

Coordination of vessel requirements across operational, administrative and commercial interfaces.

  • Vessel operations coordination
  • Maintenance requirements
  • Inspections and surveys
  • Regulatory interfaces

Vessel chartering

Support in identifying and coordinating chartering requirements based on operational needs and available vessel capability.

  • Requirement assessment
  • Vessel identification
  • Market availability engagement
  • Commercial coordination

Offshore & logistics

Moving people, equipment and materials

Offshore logistics

Support for the movement of people, equipment, materials and vessels required for offshore and marine-dependent activities.

  • Marine transportation coordination
  • Offshore supply support
  • Port interface
  • Vessel mobilisation

Supply chain & procurement

Sourcing and procurement of equipment, materials and services for marine, offshore and industrial requirements.

  • Marine and technical equipment
  • Spare parts sourcing
  • Safety-related equipment
  • Commercial comparison and negotiation

Marine & technical support

Coordination of specialist marine and technical requirements through qualified providers and partners.

  • Vessel repairs
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Dry-docking support
  • Inspection and survey coordination

Marine security & technology

What we work with

  • Maritime awareness
  • Vessel visibility
  • Security monitoring
  • Situational awareness
  • Decision support
  • System integration
