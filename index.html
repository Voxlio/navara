Who we are

Built around marine operations

Navara Offshore is a marine and offshore company focused on the management, movement and support of marine assets and offshore activities.

We work across vessel operations, fleet management, chartering, offshore logistics, procurement, technical support and maritime technology — bringing together the people, resources and coordination required to keep operations moving.

Our approach is practical and commercially minded. We look beyond the vessel itself to understand the wider operation, the client's objectives and the environment in which the work is being performed.
